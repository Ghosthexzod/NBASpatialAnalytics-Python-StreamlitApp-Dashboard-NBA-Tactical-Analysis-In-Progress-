6. Convex Hull Team Spacing Analysis

One of the most advanced parts of the project was Convex Hull analysis.

What is Convex Hull?
Convex Hull creates a polygon around all player positions to measure:
- offensive spread
- defensive compactness
- team spacing

Main Metrics:
- Team spatial area
- Offensive spread
- Defensive density

Key Findings:
- Lakers showed larger spatial spread
- Celtics were more compact defensively
- Larger convex hull areas suggested wider spacing

Basketball Insight:
Wide spacing improves:
- passing lanes
- driving opportunities
- shooting space

Compact defensive shapes help:
- protect the paint
- reduce passing angles
- limit offensive penetration

This type of analysis is commonly used in:
- professional sports analytics
- tactical performance analysis
- team shape modelling
